The Last Territory

How highways, development, and habitat loss reshaped the Florida panther’s survival.

Once found throughout the southeastern United States, the Florida panther’s range extended across millions of acres of forest and wetland. By the mid-20th century, hunting and rapid urban expansion reduced the population to fewer than 30 individuals.

Today, recovery efforts have increased population numbers — but geographic space remains limited. Road mortality has become one of the leading documented causes of death.
