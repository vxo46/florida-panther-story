From Collapse to Fragile Recovery

1970s: Near Extinction

Population estimates suggest only 20–30 panthers remained in the wild.

1995: Genetic Rescue

Texas cougars were introduced to increase genetic diversity.

2000s: Recovery

Population estimates began steadily rising across South Florida.

Today: Fragile Stability

Modern estimates range between 120–230 panthers, though habitat remains limited.

Source: Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission population estimates.
