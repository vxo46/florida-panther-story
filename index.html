The Last Territory

How highways and development reshaped the Florida panther’s survival.

A Species Reduced to Space

Once roaming across the southeastern United States, the Florida panther’s range contracted steadily through the twentieth century. Habitat loss and road networks pushed the species into isolated fragments of Southwest Florida.

Highways as Barriers

Vehicle collisions are now the leading documented cause of panther mortality. Wildlife underpasses reduce deaths, but habitat connectivity remains fragile.
